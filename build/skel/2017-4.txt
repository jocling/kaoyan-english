chunks Though often viewed as a problem for western states,〔C1〕 the growing frequency of wildfires is a national concern〔主〕 because of its impact on federal tax dollars,〔修〕 says Professor Max Moritz, a specialist in fire ecology and management.〔修〕

mod of wildfires → frequency | 介词短语作定语 | 山火的频率
mod because of its impact on federal tax dollars → is a national concern | 原因状语 | 因为它影响联邦税款
mod says Professor Max Moritz → 整句 | 引语标注（倒装） | 正常语序是 Max Moritz says
mod a specialist in fire ecology and management → Professor Max Moritz | 同位语 | 他是火灾生态与管理专家
